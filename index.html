CampusCycle Project Summary

A Circular Campus Marketplace for Affordable Learning Materials at Universiti Malaysia Kelantan (UMK)

02. Problem Statements

  • High learning costs for university students per semester.
  • Usable textbooks remain idle after semester completion.
  • Fragmented and inconvenient buying/selling channels.
  • Limited verification, tracking, and pickup collection points.

03. CampusCycle Solution

  • Circular Economy: Read -> Reuse -> Relearn -> Recycle.
  • Smart Lockers: Contactless 24/7 pickup at UMK Library.
  • Affordable Access: Buy, Rent, Sell, or Bundle textbooks.
  • Eco-Incentives: Earn CampusCycle Coins for discounts.

04. 5-Step Project Methodology

1

Resource Identification

Collect textbooks, past papers, notes.

2

Marketplace Setup

Structured mobile & web listings.

3

Pilot Launch

20-30 books at UMK Library hub.

4

Incentives & Coins

Eco-rewards & voucher partners.

5

Evaluation

Savings & feasibility metrics.
